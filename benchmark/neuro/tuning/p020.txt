[[Oswestry Disability Index 52%]]. [[VAS leg 8/10]], back 4/10. No [[saddle anaesthesia]] or [[bladder dysfunction]].

She has failed [[epidural steroid injection]] x2 and physiotherapy. I have recommended [[L3-5 decompressive laminectomy]] with [[bilateral partial medial facetectomy]] and [[foraminotomy]], without fusion. Alternative: [[unilateral laminotomy for bilateral decompression (ULBD)]].
