PREOPERATIVE DIAGNOSIS: Right [[parasagittal meningioma]], middle third of the [[superior sagittal sinus]], 4.1 cm.
POSTOPERATIVE DIAGNOSIS: Same; [[WHO grade 1 meningioma]] on frozen section.
PROCEDURE: [[Right parasagittal craniotomy]] with [[image-guided resection]] using [[Medtronic StealthStation S8]], [[Simpson grade II]] resection (tumour removed, dural attachment coagulated, sinus wall left in situ).

INDICATIONS: 62-year-old man with progressive [[left leg weakness]] and two [[focal aware seizures]]. Preoperative [[MR venography]] showed the [[superior sagittal sinus]] patent but partially invaded. [[Preoperative embolization]] was not performed.

DESCRIPTION: After induction, the head was fixed in a [[Mayfield three-pin skull clamp]]. Neuronavigation registration error 1.2 mm. A [[horseshoe incision]] was made. Burr holes were placed with the [[Midas Rex perforator]] and the bone flap turned with the [[B1 craniotome footplate]]. The dura was opened in a C-shape based on the sinus. The tumour was debulked with the [[CUSA ultrasonic aspirator]] (amplitude 70%) and dissected off the [[motor cortex]] under the [[Zeiss Kinevo operating microscope]], preserving the [[arachnoid plane]] and the draining [[Rolandic vein]]. [[Motor evoked potentials]] remained stable throughout. The sinus wall attachment was coagulated with [[bipolar cautery]]. Dural defect repaired with [[DuraGen dural graft matrix]] and [[DuraSeal]]. Flap secured with [[titanium plates]].

EBL 450 mL. No complications.
