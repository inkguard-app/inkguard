# Guides

One guide per question people ask before they trust a de-identification
tool. Each follows the same structure:

- **At a glance**: the facts in one table.
- **The standard**: the regulation, guidance or accepted practice that
  applies, with its source.
- **Inkguard's position**: what the software does, stated as facts.
- **What it needs from you**: what your team must provide for the standard
  to be met.
- **What it does not do**: limits, and what is planned.

| Guide | Question |
|---|---|
| [Private by design](privacy.md) | Where are records processed, and who else is involved? |
| [HIPAA Safe Harbor](safe-harbor.md) | What does it look for, and what remains your decision? |
| [Human review](review.md) | Who checks the output, and who decides? (Describes the review workflow in development.) |
| [Accuracy](accuracy.md) | How much is removed, and how much clinical detail is kept? |
| [Reproducibility and citation](reproducibility.md) | Can a published result be checked later, and how is it cited? |

Figures refer to version 0.25.0 (public preview). None of these guides is
legal advice; your privacy officer, IRB or ethics board, and counsel decide
what your organisation requires.
