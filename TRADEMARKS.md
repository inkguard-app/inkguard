So that "Inkguard" always means the version maintained here, and its
published results always describe that version:

- If you share a changed copy, please give it a different name and logo.
- You are welcome to say it is "based on Inkguard" and to link here.
- Unchanged copies may keep the name.

Results measured on a changed copy are results for that copy, not for
Inkguard. Each Inkguard report carries a fingerprint of the exact code that
produced it.
